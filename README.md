# government-analytics-powerbi-dashboards
# 📊 Government Analytics Dashboard Suite (Power BI)

## 🚀 Overview

This project includes **6 Power BI dashboards** that I created while working as a freelancer on a government project.

The goal was to take raw data and turn it into **simple and useful insights** for areas like **education, water supply, agriculture, and public services**.

---

## 📊 What Each Dashboard Does

### 🔹 SBM MR 3C

* Shows sanitation-related data
* Helps find areas where work is incomplete

### 🔹 School and Meal Served Status

* Tracks meal distribution in schools
* Shows how well schools are participating

### 🔹 Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM)

* Shows water supply coverage
* Tracks progress of household connections

### 🔹 Krushi Yojana

* Shows how agricultural schemes are performing
* Tracks how many people are benefiting

### 🔹 School Enrollment – Category Wise

* Shows student enrollment by category and gender
* Helps find gaps in education

### 🔹 Drinking Water

* Shows availability of drinking water
* Compares performance across regions

---

## 🎯 Purpose of This Project

* To make government data easy to understand
* To find problems and gaps in services
* To help in better planning and decisions
* To track performance in different areas

---

## 📈 Key Learnings / Insights

* Some regions are not getting equal resources
* Certain groups have lower school enrollment
* Water supply is strong in some areas but weak in others
* Benefits of schemes are not reaching everyone equally
